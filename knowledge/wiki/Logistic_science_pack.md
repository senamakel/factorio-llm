# Logistic science pack

Source: https://wiki.factorio.com/Logistic_science_pack (revision 218028)

## Infobox
- Required technologies: Logistic science pack
- Prototype type: tool
- Internal (prototype) name: logistic-science-pack
- Category: Intermediate products
- Stack size: 200
- Science capacity: 100% (by quality normal/uncommon/rare/epic/legendary: 100% / 200% / 300% / 400% / 600%)
- Extra1: Used by labs for research.
- Recipe: 6 s; 1 × Inserter + 1 × Transport belt
- Total raw (per craft): 8.75 s; 1.5 × Copper plate + 5.5 × Iron plate
- Producers: Assembling machine, Player
- Rocket capacity: 1000 (5 stacks)

**Logistic science pack** (also referred to as "green science") is the second tier of science pack. It is used in labs to research technologies.
