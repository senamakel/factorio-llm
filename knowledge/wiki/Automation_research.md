# Automation (research)

Source: https://wiki.factorio.com/Automation_(research) (revision 218141)

## Infobox
- Required technologies: Automation science pack
- Prototype type: technology
- Internal (prototype) name: automation
- Category: Technology
- Research cost per unit: 10 s; 1 × Automation science pack
- Research units (count): 10
- Allows (unlocks follow-up techs): Automation 2, 2
- Effects (unlocks): Assembling machine 1, Long-handed inserter
- Extra1: Key technology for large scale production.

**Automation** is one of the first technologies that the player will research. It allows the building of the Long-handed inserter, as well as the Assembling machine 1, which is the start of automated crafting, an essential part of Factorio.

The cost of this research is not affected by the technology price multiplier set during map creation.
