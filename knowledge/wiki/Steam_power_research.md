# Steam power (research)

Source: https://wiki.factorio.com/Steam_power_(research) (revision 202555)

## Infobox
- Research trigger (unlocked by doing this, no science packs): craft-item: Iron plate × 50
- Category: Technology
- Prototype type: technology
- Internal (prototype) name: steam-power
- Allows (unlocks follow-up techs): Automation science pack
- Effects (unlocks): Boiler, Offshore pump, Pipe, Pipe to ground, Steam engine

The **Steam power** research allows the player to build the necessary buildings to produce power in early game. It allows the crafting and construction of the boiler, offshore pump, pipe, pipe to ground, and steam engine.
