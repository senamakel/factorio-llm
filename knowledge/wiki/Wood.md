**Wood** is a basic resource that can be collected by harvesting trees, water cane(Space Age), boompuff(Space Age), cuttlepop(Space Age) and stingfrond(Space Age). Wood can be used in crafting recipes but can also be used as fuel in all burner devices as well as in cars, tanks and locomotives. The player starts with 1 wood when starting a freeplay game.

- Dead trees will give 2 wood.
- Big trees will give 4 wood.

## Trivia
- In the base game, wood is the only crafting material that cannot be harvested or produced using automation.
- Using the starter wood, it is possible to win the game without enabling trees.
