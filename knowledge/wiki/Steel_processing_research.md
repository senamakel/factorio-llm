# Steel processing (research)

Source: https://wiki.factorio.com/Steel_processing_(research) (revision 189295)

## Infobox
- Required technologies: Automation science pack
- Prototype type: technology
- Internal (prototype) name: steel-processing
- Category: Technology
- Research cost per unit: 5 s; 1 × Automation science pack
- Research units (count): 50
- Allows (unlocks follow-up techs): Advanced material processing, Automation 2, 2, Electric energy distribution 1, 1, Engine, Heavy armor, Military 2, 2, Solar energy, Steel axe
- Effects (unlocks): Steel chest, Steel plate
- Extra1: Allows the smelting of iron plates into steel bars.

**Steel processing** allows you to process steel from iron plates, and enables the creation of basic steel items. Due to the large quantity of research options unlocked and how easy it is to obtain, this will be one of the first technologies that the player researches.
