# Military science pack

Source: https://wiki.factorio.com/Military_science_pack (revision 219731)

## Infobox
- Prototype type: tool
- Internal (prototype) name: military-science-pack
- Category: Intermediate products
- Stack size: 200
- Science capacity: 100% (by quality normal/uncommon/rare/epic/legendary: 100% / 200% / 300% / 400% / 600%)
- Extra1: Used by labs for research.
- Recipe: 10 s; 1 × Grenade + 1 × Piercing rounds magazine + 2 × Wall -> 2 × Military science pack
- Total raw (per craft): 23 s; 10 × Coal + 1 × Copper plate + 9 × Iron plate + 0.5 × Steel plate + 10 × Stone brick
- Producers: Assembling machine, Player
- Required technologies: Military science pack
- Rocket capacity: 1000 (5 stacks)

The **Military science pack** is one of the four early game science packs, the other three being the automation science pack, the logistic science pack, and the chemical science pack.
It is used for research that allows the player to advance their military strength through the entire game, unlocking more bullet choices, gun choices, turrets, etc. It is the only type of science pack that is optional, in that it is not required to launch a rocket.
