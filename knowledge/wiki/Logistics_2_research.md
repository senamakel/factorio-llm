# Logistics 2 (research)

Source: https://wiki.factorio.com/Logistics_2_(research) (revision 147095)

## Infobox
- Prototype type: technology
- Internal (prototype) name: logistics-2
- Category: Technology
- Icontext: 2
- Research cost per unit: 30 s; 1 × Automation science pack + 1 × Logistic science pack
- Research units (count): 200
- Required technologies: Logistic science pack, Logistics
- Allows (unlocks follow-up techs): Automobilism, Bulk inserter, Railway
- Effects (unlocks): Fast splitter, Fast transport belt, Fast underground belt

**Logistics 2** allows the player to research faster methods of transportation, such as the locomotive, and the Car. The Fast transport belt is also unlocked.
