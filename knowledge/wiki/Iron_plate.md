The **iron plate** is a material that can be made by smelting iron ore in a furnace, or cast by a foundry(Space Age).  It is a component or subcomponent of almost every item in Factorio, which is why it makes sense to ensure a continuous, uninterrupted supply in sufficient quantities. When smelted in a furnace for a second time, iron plates will transform into steel plates, with a ratio of 5 iron plates per 1 steel plate.

When starting a new freeplay game, the player starts out with 8 iron plates, along with 1 burner mining drill and 1 stone furnace.
