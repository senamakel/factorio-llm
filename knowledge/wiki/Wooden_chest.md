# Wooden chest

Source: https://wiki.factorio.com/Wooden_chest (revision 211603)

## Infobox
- Mining time (to pick up): 0.1
- Prototype type: container
- Internal (prototype) name: wooden-chest
- Category: Logistics
- Recipe: 0.5 s; 2 × Wood
- Producers: Assembling machine, Player
- Health: 100 (by quality normal/uncommon/rare/epic/legendary: 100 / 130 / 160 / 190 / 250)
- Rocket capacity: 50
- Dimensions: 1×1
- Stack size: 50
- Storage size: 16 (by quality normal/uncommon/rare/epic/legendary: 16 / 20 / 25 / 30 / 40)

**Wooden chests** are the first type of chest available to the player at the start of the game. They are, like any other means of storage, used for storing items at a fixed position and can be connected to the circuit network to provide their contents as a signal.

## History

Version 0.13.0:
