# Chemical science pack

Source: https://wiki.factorio.com/Chemical_science_pack (revision 218030)

## Infobox
- Prototype type: tool
- Internal (prototype) name: chemical-science-pack
- Category: Intermediate products
- Stack size: 200
- Science capacity: 100% (by quality normal/uncommon/rare/epic/legendary: 100% / 200% / 300% / 400% / 600%)
- Extra1: Used by labs for research.
- Recipe: 24 s; 3 × Advanced circuit + 2 × Engine unit + 1 × Sulfur -> 2 × Chemical science pack
- Total raw (per craft): 52.5 s; 15 × Copper plate + 2 × Engine unit + 6 × Iron plate + 6 × Plastic bar + 1 × Sulfur
- Required technologies: Chemical science pack
- Producers: Assembling machine, Player
- Rocket capacity: 1000 (5 stacks)

**Chemical science pack** (also referred to as "blue science") is the third tier of science pack. It is used in labs to research advanced technologies.
