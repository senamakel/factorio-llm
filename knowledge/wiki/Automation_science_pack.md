# Automation science pack

Source: https://wiki.factorio.com/Automation_science_pack (revision 218027)

- Category: Intermediate products
- Stack size: 200
- Extra1: Used by labs for research.
- Science capacity: 100% (by quality normal/uncommon/rare/epic/legendary: 100% / 200% / 300% / 400% / 600%)
- Recipe: 5 s; 1 × Copper plate + 1 × Iron gear wheel
- Total raw (per craft): 5.5 s; 1 × Copper plate + 2 × Iron plate
- Producers: Assembling machine, Player
- Rocket capacity: 1000 (5 stacks)

**Automation science pack** (also referred to as "red science") is the first tier of science pack. It is used in labs to research technologies.
