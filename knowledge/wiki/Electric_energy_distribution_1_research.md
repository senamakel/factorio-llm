# Electric energy distribution 1 (research)

Source: https://wiki.factorio.com/Electric_energy_distribution_1_(research) (revision 146840)

## Infobox
- Prototype type: technology
- Internal (prototype) name: electric-energy-distribution-1
- Category: Technology
- Research cost per unit: 30 s; 1 × Automation science pack + 1 × Logistic science pack
- Research units (count): 120
- Required technologies: Logistic science pack, Steel processing
- Allows (unlocks follow-up techs): Electric energy accumulators, Electric energy distribution 2, 2
- Effects (unlocks): Big electric pole, Iron stick, Medium electric pole

**Electric energy distribution 1** gives the player access to bigger power poles, very useful for transferring power over a greater distance. It also unlocks Electric energy accumulators (research), essential for a pollution-less power generating system.
